ABOUT APP

Your Eyes Smart Cane v2.2

An assistive app for visually impaired people. It uses the camera to recognise common obstacles (people, vehicles, furniture, animals) and tells their side and approximate distance. Created by Humaira Sardar, Okara City, Pakistan.

Important: this app does not replace a white cane or a guide. It cannot detect stairs, pits, walls, glass or poles, and distances are only estimates. The "Ask the Smart Cane" button sends one camera photo to an online AI service.
